## 1. One-line product

**A latticework of powerful ideas** — every idea (mental model _and_ primary-source
document) is a **Node** with 3 layered teachings (`layer0/1/2`), a quiz, related
cross-links, and curated `furtherReading` with an offline-archived snapshot.
Learn → layer reveal → quiz → Leitner spaced repetition → streak. Source of
truth: `docs/PRODUCT-BRIEF.md`.

**Design invariant:** _If a feature doesn't help an idea connect to another
idea, or help it stick in memory, it doesn't belong._
